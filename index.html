Autoclave

1. Vertical Autoclave

Usage: Laboratory, Clinics, and small healthcare centers.
Mechanism: Steam under pressure (Gravity displacement).
Key Features: Top-loading cylindrical chamber, Stainless steel construction, Ideal for culture media. Primary Function: The main purpose of this machine is sterilization. It uses high-pressure steam to effectively eliminate bacteria, viruses, fungi, and spores from medical instruments, laboratory glassware, and other materials.

2. Horizontal Autoclave

Usage: Large Hospitals, CSSD, and O.T.
Mechanism: Pre-vacuum steam sterilization for deep penetration.
Key Features: Front-loading rectangular chamber, High capacity for bulk sets, Faster drying cycle.

3. Sterile Dilator Set

Indicator Tape ka kaam: Ye tape sterilization ke dauran heat aur pressure ke sampark mein aane par apna rang badal leti hai. Ye is baat ka proof hota hai ki instruments ke set ko sahi tareeke se autoclave process se gujara gaya hai.
Surgical instruments ko pehle ek saaf kapde mein wrap kiya jata hai taaki sterilization ke baad wo contamination se safe rahein.

4. Autoclave Trolley

This trolley is specifically designed for use with horizontal autoclave machines. Wrapped instrument sets are placed onto this trolley, which is then slid into the horizontal chamber of the autoclave for the sterilization process. Constructed from stainless steel with perforated trays for proper steam circulation.

5. Sterilization Process

Cleaning and Drying → Packaging → Labeling with indicator tape → Loading on trolley → Autoclave Cycle (pre-vacuum steam) → Fast drying cycle. This ensures instruments meet hospital infection control standards.

ETO Sterilizer

Usage: Heat & moisture sensitive instruments (plastic, rubber, electronics).
Mechanism: Ethylene Oxide gas sterilization (not steam).
Key Point: Used when autoclave cannot be used because high heat/moisture would damage the item.

Packaging for ETO

Dual-layer medical pouches (plastic + breathable paper) allow ETO gas to penetrate while maintaining sterile barrier after process.

Ultrasonic Cleaner

Function: Deep cleaning of instruments using ultrasonic waves before sterilization.
Important: Never operate without water. Controls include power, ultrasonic on/off, heater and digital timer.

Store Lobby

Sterile storage area for processed instrument sets. Maintain proper temperature, humidity and First-In-First-Out (FIFO) system. Only authorized staff should enter.
← Back

Washing Area

Area for manual + ultrasonic cleaning of used instruments. Strict separation of dirty and clean zones is mandatory to prevent cross-contamination.
← Back

Sterilization Zone

Controlled area where autoclaves, ETO and other sterilizers are operated. Only trained CSSD staff allowed. Proper PPE and monitoring of cycles is essential.
