← Back

Autoclave

1. Vertical Autoclave

Usage: Laboratory, Clinics, and small healthcare centers.
Mechanism: Steam under pressure (Gravity displacement).
Key Features: Top-loading cylindrical chamber, Stainless steel construction, Ideal for culture media and instruments.

2. Horizontal Autoclave

Usage: Large Hospitals, CSSD, and O.T.
Mechanism: Pre-vacuum steam sterilization for deep penetration.
Key Features: Front-loading rectangular chamber, High capacity, Faster drying cycle.
← Back

ETO Sterilizer

Usage: Heat & moisture sensitive instruments (plastic, rubber, electronics).
Mechanism: Ethylene Oxide gas sterilization.
Key Point: Used when autoclave (steam) cannot be used.
← Back

Ultrasonic Cleaner

Function: Deep cleaning of instruments using ultrasonic waves before sterilization.
Important: Never operate without water.
← Back

Store Lobby

Sterile storage area for processed instrument sets. Maintain proper temperature, humidity and first-in-first-out system.
← Back

Washing Area

Area for manual + ultrasonic cleaning of used instruments. Strict separation of dirty and clean zones is mandatory.
← Back

Sterilization Zone

Controlled area where autoclaves, ETO and other sterilizers are operated. Only trained staff allowed.
